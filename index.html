What we set up

Three services, or the whole platform.

  1. 01

    Renewal Autopilot

    Reminders 30, 14, 7 and 3 days before a plan ends, and on the day, each with a link to renew. Lapsed members get a short series of "we miss you" messages.

  2. 02

    AI Front Desk

    Answers prices, opening hours, trials and classes on WhatsApp and your website, at any hour. It answers only from what you give it and hands over to a person when unsure.

  3. 03

    Website that books trials

    A fast, mobile-first site in your brand where visitors see your plans, book a trial and join online, with every enquiry landing in one place.

  4. 04

    Member app

    Part of the full platform: memberships, class and PT bookings, check-in and payments, in your gym's name and colours.

  5. 05

    Front desk & trainers

    Check-in, attendance and member records for the desk, and a trainer portal for schedules, clients and workouts.

  6. 06

    Owner dashboard

    Payments, renewals due, leads and reports in one place, across one branch or several. Payments go to your own account.

One example: the AI Front Desk

01

A prospect writes in at 11 pm.

Prices, trials, timings. If the reply comes the next afternoon, they have often joined somewhere else.

02

The AI reads it.

It works out what they want and when. It answers only from your gym's own information and never invents prices or timings.

03

A reply goes out in seconds.

Short, friendly and from your gym's name, on WhatsApp or your website. Anything it isn't sure about goes to your team.

04

You get the lead.

Name, need and a suggested next step land in your list, so your desk only steps in to close.
