FAQ

Questions gym owners ask us

Do I have to switch from my current gym software?

No. The reminders, front desk and website work alongside the software you already use, using its data where it allows or a simple export of your member list. The full platform is only for gyms that want to move everything into one system.

What does it cost?

It depends on the size of the gym and which services you want, so we give each gym a fixed quote after a 30-minute call. You pay half to start and half once it's live and you're happy with it.

Which number do the WhatsApp messages come from?

Your gym's own WhatsApp Business number, sent through WhatsApp's official business platform. Members see your gym's name, not ours.

Will my members know it's MAXD?

No. Every message, page and app screen carries your gym's name and branding.

Who can see my member data?

You, and the people who run your setup. It's only used to send your gym's messages, it's kept separate from every other gym, and you can download or delete it whenever you want.

Where are you based?

In Mysuru, India. We work with gyms in other time zones over WhatsApp and video calls.

How many members did you lose last quarter?

Most owners don't know the exact number. On a 30-minute call we'll work it out from your member list, look at where your enquiries come from, and give you a fixed quote. If none of it is worth doing for your gym, we'll tell you.
