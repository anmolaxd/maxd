Privacy Policy

Last updated 14 November 2026

This policy explains what MAXD ("we") collects when you visit this website or contact us, and how we use it. MAXD is based in Mysuru, Karnataka, India.

What we collect

  • When you contact us by WhatsApp or email: your name, phone number or email address, and what you write to us.
  • When you visit this site: nothing directly. The site has no contact forms, no analytics and no advertising cookies. Our website host and font provider (Google Fonts) may log basic technical data such as IP address, as described in their own policies.

How we use it

Only to reply to you, prepare a quote you asked for, and deliver work you have agreed to. We do not sell your information or use it for unrelated marketing.

Your gym's member data

When we set up MAXD for your gym, your member list, messages and payments data belong to you. We use them only to run your gym's setup, keep them separate from every other gym, and you can download or delete them at any time. Renewal payments go to your own payment account and never pass through us.

How long we keep it

Conversations with prospective clients are kept for up to 12 months after our last contact, unless we start working together. Project records are kept for as long as the project runs and as required by Indian law.

Your rights

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, by emailing anmolaxd@gmail.com. We will reply within 30 days. We handle personal data in line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
