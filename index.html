04 / House rules

You stay in control.

  1. Fixed price. A written quote after our call. No hourly billing.
  2. Half now, half live. The second half only once it is live and you are happy.
  3. Your yes first. Nothing reaches a member without your approval.
  4. Your brand. Members see your gym's name, never ours.
  5. Your data. Kept separate from every other gym. Download or delete it any time.
  6. Your money. Renewal payments go straight to your account, never through us.

05 / Questions

Straight answers.

Do I have to switch from my current gym software?

No. The three services work alongside the software you already use, from its data where it allows or from a simple export of your member list. The full platform is only for gyms that want one system for everything.

What does it cost?

Each gym gets a fixed quote after a 30-minute call, sized to the gym and the services you choose. You pay half to start and half once it is live and you are happy with it.

Will my members know it's MAXD?

No. Every message, page and app screen carries your gym's name and brand.

Who can see my member data?

Only you and the people who run your setup. It is used only to run your gym's messages, kept separate from every other gym, and yours to download or delete at any time.

Is the full platform live today?

The full platform is a working demo with sample data and is not yet live at a gym. Renewal Autopilot is built and tested, and runs in test mode until the owner approves it.

Where are you based?

MAXD is built by Studio AXD in Mysuru, India, and works with gyms in any time zone over WhatsApp and video calls.
